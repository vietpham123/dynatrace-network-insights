Reading it: two levers dominate the bill — NetFlow sampling (raw-netflow retention is astronomical; the bars show why) and metric poll interval (1-min across a large fleet is billions of datapoints). Metrics are cheap per unit ($0.15/100k, 15-month retention free) but scale with poll × fleet; sampled netflow is cheap, raw is not. Config/compliance is noise. Pay-per-query adds $0.0035/GiB scanned (usage-based, not shown here). GiB = 1024³ bytes, to match Dynatrace's unit.
